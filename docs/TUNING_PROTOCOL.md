# Earlier readiness tuning

`notebooks/mqr_learning_pilot.ipynb` refers to an earlier two-seed, 128-update learning pilot on the smaller readiness corpus. Its calibration results and schedule differ from the later three-seed, 1,000-update confirmation.

The [complete report](../research/report/report.pdf) describes that pilot and the later execution experiments. The final [learning protocol](../research/protocols/learning.md) concerns the separate confirmation study and should not be substituted silently for the earlier pilot's settings. Original detailed pilot records remain in the local research archive.

See [the reproduction guide](REPRODUCING.md) for the public software checks and the additional private inputs needed for full experiment repetition.
